Computer Vision ML Engineer

Munich, Germany — Hybrid

Industrial Automation | AI & Data Science | Computer Vision | Deep Learning | Industrial AI


Our client, a growing Industrial Automation company based in Munich, is looking for a Computer Vision ML Engineer to develop and optimise machine-learning models for automated inspection, visual recognition, and industrial imaging applications.


You'll work at the intersection of Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Industrial AI, building practical solutions that transform visual data into reliable, production-ready intelligence.


Core Skills

• 3+ years in Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Applied AI, or a similar role

• Strong Python development experience

• PyTorch

• OpenCV

• Deep Learning

• Computer Vision fundamentals

• C++

• Experience working with NVIDIA GPUs

• Image classification, object detection, or segmentation

• Experience developing and evaluating practical computer vision models

XpertDirect is where companies hire technical specialists directly. No agency in the middle, no recruiter deciding what anyone is worth.

An operator describes the work. Xpert AI structures it into a brief. XpertDirect's own algorithm scores every applicant against that brief, and a LinkedIn job publication opens the same brief to the specialist market. What comes back is not a longlist. It is the specialists who read that brief and confirmed they are interested and available — interest-validated, not resembling it on paper.

From there the operator talks to the specialist. Directly, in the platform, with no account manager passing messages along.

A flat 5% platform fee, charged on top of the specialist's rate. The specialist sets the rate, is paid directly, and keeps all of it.

Three families, deliberately: Data, ML & AI Engineering; Platform & Infrastructure; Security Engineering. Depth in a narrow domain is worth more to an operator than coverage everywhere.

Specialists set their own rate and keep it whole. Registration is free, appearing against a brief is free, and there is no subscription. Nobody is presented to a brief they have not seen, and nobody is quoted at a rate they did not set. Specialist data is held in the EU.

Direct. Transparent. Evidenced. Every figure we publish carries a date, a sample and a method — and where the evidence does not exist yet, we describe the system instead of implying a result.

Built for operators who need answers, not decks.
