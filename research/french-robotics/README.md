## Why this list is filtered, not adopted wholesale

Sanket is Berlin-based, English C1 / German A2 (no French), German MSc → §20 job-seeker permit → work permit **in Germany, without employer sponsorship**. A French on-site role needs French B1+ *and* French work-permit sponsorship, so it is not actionable. Actionable surface = a company with a **German entity hiring in Germany**, or a role explicitly open to **remote EU/Europe**.

Every claim below was checked against a live page by a research agent on 2026-10-21; the per-company evidence, quotes and source URLs are in `sources/`.

## The Genesis AI caveat (worth a decision)

Genesis AI is the only company in the list that matches Sanket's *technical* profile as squarely as the location filter allows: Genesis World is a multi-physics simulation engine with a GPU path-traced renderer, used to train embodied AI — the same domain as the Webots/ROS2/Nav2 work. Its London roles (Core Simulation Engine, Robot Learning, Foundation Models, Training/AI Infrastructure, Simulation Infrastructure, ML Compiler) are the closest thing to a dream-role match in the whole post.

They are **not surfaced today**, for a reason that predates this sweep: `portals.yml` has `United Kingdom` in `location_filter.negative` and no `London` in the positive list, so every London posting is dropped. That was a deliberate 2026-10-20 change to the filter. Surfacing UK on-site roles would mean (a) editing the location filter, and (b) deciding that a UK relocation is worth the sponsorship burden — his German permit does not transfer. This sweep did not make that decision; it wired the board so that any *genuinely remote* role lands immediately, and the entry's `notes` say so.
