# Berlin AI/Retail/Logistics Employer Sweep — Reverse (job-board → employer)

**Method:** searched *job boards* (StepStone, Bundesagentur für Arbeit / Jobsuche, Indeed DE, LinkedIn Jobs, XING Jobs, JOIN, SmartRecruiters, Greenhouse, Ashby, Recruitee, Berlin Startup Jobs, Welcome to the Jungle/Otta, Wellfound, RapidJob, Built In, startup.jobs) for Berlin AI/ML/data roles using both German and English retail/logistics/inventory vocabulary, then identified the employers behind the postings and verified each employer's Berlin presence, business, and size.

**Cut-off / freshness:** all postings below were seen live between 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-20 unless a different date is stated. "Seen" dates are the board's own `releasedDate` / "Vor X Tagen veröffentlicht" field. Nothing here is inferred — every employer below has at least one job posting I actually retrieved, plus a company source I actually retrieved.

**Filter applied:** Berlin location on the posting (Berlin city, or "Berlin" listed as an eligible location for a multi-city posting) + the role is AI/ML/CV/data + retail / inventory / warehousing / logistics / grocery / e-commerce / marketplace / supply-chain relevance.

### Method notes / caveats
- **Discovery**: `websearch` + Exa `exa_search` (live crawl) across StepStone, Bundesagentur Jobsuche (incl. its own search result pages), Indeed DE, LinkedIn Jobs, XING Jobs, JOIN, SmartRecruiters, Greenhouse (incl. EU boards), Ashby, Recruitee, Berlin Startup Jobs, Welcome to the Jungle/Otta, Wellfound, RapidJob, Built In, startup.jobs, Qarera, DEVjobs, findjob24h, Dr.Job, JobTarget, ClearanceJobs.
- **Live-status verification**: for companies on SmartRecruiters I pulled `api.smartrecruiters.com/v1/companies/{slug}/postings` (real `releasedDate` + city), which is why Flink and Delivery Hero entries carry exact 2026-10-xx release dates rather than "X days ago".
- **Ambiguity flagged, not hidden**: Delivery Hero and Vinted postings frequently appear both live and as "expired" on different mirrors — where that happened I list every URL I saw and mark confidence Medium.
- **Not included in core**: employers whose only Berlin roles are sales/ops/warehouse (no AI/data), employers whose AI roles are travel/adtech/health/fintech/consulting (moved to Adjacent), and any employer for which I could not find a live posting (moved to the watch list).
- **Naming caution**: "Clera" is ambiguous — the Berlin entry here is the wholesale-distribution AI company (ashby board), not the US-HQ "Clera" talent agent (getclera.com).
