# Agent 4 — Berlin AI × Retail / Inventory / Warehousing / Logistics
## Discovery via curated directories, ecosystem maps, VC/accelerator portfolios and trade press

**Method:** I crawled curated, Berlin-specific ecosystem directories end-to-end (ai.berlin official AI
ecosystem directory — all **876** entries; Seedtable "Berlin Startup Map" — 329 companies; TechInBerlin
category directories) and cross-checked each candidate against VC/accelerator portfolios (IBB Ventures,
Fraunhofer Venture), trade press (Handelsblatt, Sifted, EU-Startups, tech.eu, openPR/DE) and company
profiles. Every company below has at least one source URL that was actually fetched.

**Filter applied:** Berlin HQ (or a real Berlin engineering/tech office) **AND** the AI/ML work touches
retail, inventory, warehousing, logistics, supply chain, procurement, shop-floor / shelf / product imagery,
or the software layer that runs those processes.
