## Methodology & coverage notes

- Search engines used: **exa** (`category:"company"`, `type:"auto"`, `type:"keyword"`, incl. `site:linkedin.com/company`, `site:crunchbase.com`), plus **websearch**. German queries run: *"Lagerverwaltung KI Berlin Unternehmen Software"*, *"Bestandsprognose Unternehmen Berlin Software"*, *"Logistik Robotik Berlin Startup Intralogistik"*, *"Intralogistik KI"*, *"Kommissionierroboter Berlin Unternehmen"*, *"Wareneingang OCR Logistik Berlin Software"*, *"Bestandsmanagement Software KI Berlin"*, *"Supply Chain AI Berlin"*, *"Lager Robotik Berlin Startup deeptech"*, *"Lagerbestandsoptimierung Berlin"*.
- ATS platforms read directly: **Personio** (Plato, traide AI, Mercanis, Alpas DE), **Ashby** (Bettermile, Zauber, cargo.one), **Needle** (Delvo, GeneralMind), **SmartRecruiters** (akirolabs), **join.com** (Logistica OS, Codept, senvo, opus, ctrl+s, RoboService), **Rippling ATS** (Hive FC BER GmbH), **Recruitee** (AddressHub).
- Employee counts / founding years come from LinkedIn/Tracxn-derived company profiles surfaced via exa (marked with confidence); product claims come from the companies' own sites (fetched) — where a fact is only in the profile, the confidence is lowered.
