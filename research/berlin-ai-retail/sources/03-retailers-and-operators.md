## Method / reproducibility notes

- **Primary ATS APIs fetched** (all returned 200 with data): Greenhouse `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{t}/jobs` (caronsale, hellofresh, autoscout24, awin, getyourguide, commercetools, wolt), SmartRecruiters `api.smartrecruiters.com/v1/companies/{t}/postings` (Flink3, ABOUTYOUGmbH, Redcare-Pharmacy, home24, DeliveryHero, RocketInternet), Ashby `api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{t}` (voize, yazio, GotPhoto, keyrock, choco, duvo, flink, reb(u)y), Lever `api.lever.co/v0/postings/emma-sleep`, Workday `wday/cxs/{tenant}/{site}/jobs` (Zalando, Eventim, Mister Spex), Recruitee (rebuy), Recruitee-hosted idealo (`jobs.idealo.com/o/...`), Vinted's own careers Next.js payload (`careers.vinted.com/jobs?location=berlin`).
- **Berlin-presence evidence** was taken from German legal Impressum pages (fetched), company careers/about pages, exa company org profiles, and ATS location fields — never from job boards alone.
- **German-language queries used**: "KI Einzelhandel Berlin Unternehmen Machine Learning Team", "Kleinanzeigen Karriere Stellenangebote Berlin AI", "Händler Berlin Logistik KI", "Top 200 Arbeitgeber Berlin".
- **Snapshot caveat:** job boards move daily. Re-check each ATS board before applying.
