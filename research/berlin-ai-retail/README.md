## Method

Discovery ran 7 independent sweeps over different source families, then the result was cross-checked against two Berlin-specific indices:

- **ai.berlin** ecosystem directory — full catalogue (875 entries) filtered across Retail/Media, SCM & Distribution, Logistics, Operations, Forecasting, Computer Vision, Robotics, Purchasing.
- **databerlin.net/companies** — 371 Berlin employers with open data/AI roles; used as a recall check to find companies the keyword sweeps missed.
- **Seedtable "Berlin Startup Map"**, **TechInBerlin** category directories (Logistics, E-Commerce, Trade, Foodtech, AI, Robotics, Circular Economy).
- **IBB Ventures / Fraunhofer Venture / OTTO DOCK 6** portfolios, eChai *Berlin AI*, `awesome-machine-learning-startups-berlin`.
- **Trade press** — Handelsblatt, Sifted, EU-Startups, tech.eu, logistik-nachrichten.de, SupplyManagement, Gründerszene.
- **Job boards & ATS** — Arbeitsagentur, StepStone, LinkedIn, BerlinStartupJobs, Honeypot, and direct ATS boards (Personio, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters, join.com, Onlyfy, Recruitee, Coda, Teamtailor, Workday, Softgarden).
- **startupjobs.de MCP** (German DeepTech whitelist) — machine-readable Berlin/AI/ML job feed; independently confirmed Brighter AI.

Every company carries source URLs in `sources/`. `status_label` in the CSV is a live HTTP check taken at generation time.

## Caveats

- **This is a high-recall list, not provably exhaustive.** New Berlin startups appear weekly; re-run discovery quarterly.
- **"Berlin" = HQ in Berlin *or* a Berlin office doing the AI/engineering work.** A handful are registered in Berlin but operate elsewhere (Logistica OS → Bonn, Matteo → Karstädt); these are flagged in the evidence column.
- **Several entries are group-registered in Berlin without a Berlin tech team** (Back Market → Paris, Refurbished → Vienna, XXXLdigital → Wels, Unzer → Hamburg). Berlin roles exist but are not Berlin-headquartered.
- **"No careers page found" is a real signal**, not a research gap — most are 2–15 person firms that hire by referral.
- **Careers URLs rot.** Personio/Ashby/Lever boards get replaced during re-platforming, and `403`/`401` responses usually mean "changed", not "closed".
